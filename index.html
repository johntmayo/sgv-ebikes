You probably qualify.

Altadena residents are explicitly listed as eligible communities for the GoSGV Voucher Program. The next pre-application review is May 17, 2026 — apply now.

Apply for Voucher →
  • Reside in Altadena or another listed SGV community
  • Must be 18+ and not have previously received a GoSGV voucher
  • Complete a safety training course with ActiveSGV after selection
  • Participate in a UC Tennessee research study (surveys + OpenPATH app)
  • Redeem voucher at an approved retailer within 28 days of award
How it works

From application to riding

01
Submit Pre-Application

Answer basic eligibility questions online. No documents needed yet. Next review: May 17, 2026 at noon.

02
Get Selected

SGVCOG reviews applications and selects voucher recipients. If demand exceeds supply, selections are random.

03
Complete Post-App

Upload documents, attend the ActiveSGV safety course, and complete the intake survey within 28 days.

04
Ride Your Bike

Redeem your $2,000 voucher at an approved retailer within 28 days. The discount applies at point of sale — no reimbursement needed.

How this site was made

This guide was built by a local Altadena resident using Perplexity AI. The program information comes directly from the SGVCOG GoSGV Voucher Program page and the official eligible bike list.

The featured bike recommendations are not official SGVCOG endorsements. They were selected by cross-referencing the full approved bike list against independent reviews and testing from Car & Driver, OutdoorGearLab, Electric Bike Report, Bicycling Magazine, Upway, GearJunkie, and Velo/Outside, as well as community sentiment from r/CargoBike. Bikes were prioritized based on review consensus, real-world utility, and value after the $2,000 voucher is applied.

The HTML, design, and copy were generated through a conversation with Perplexity AI and refined with manual edits. Program details — deadlines, eligibility rules, retailer addresses — were verified against official SGVCOG sources at the time of writing (May 2026), but may change. Always confirm with SGVCOG directly before applying.
